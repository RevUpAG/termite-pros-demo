Know the warning signs

They've been eating long before you noticed.

Miami's heat and humidity keep termites busy all year. If you spot any of these, call for an inspection before the damage spreads.

1
Tiny pelletsSawdust-like droppings near windowsills, baseboards or furniture.
2
Discarded wingsSmall piles of wings by windows and doors after a swarm.
3
Hollow woodFrames or trim that sound hollow or crumble when pressed.
4
Mud tubesPencil-wide tunnels along the foundation or walls.
